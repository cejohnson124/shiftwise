# Ad script

**INT. PIZZA SHOP BACK OFFICE – NIGHT**

*Tense detective-show music. A fluorescent light buzzes.*

A MANAGER stares at a wall covered in sticky notes, printed emails and napkins, all connected with red string. One napkin just says "NOT TUES??"

> **MANAGER** *(whispering, wide-eyed)*
> Okay. Jordan can't do Tuesdays… unless it's *every other* Tuesday…

MARCUS, a delivery driver, pokes his head in the door.

> **MARCUS**
> Hey, you got my email about Saturday, right? My sister's wedding?

The manager slowly turns to the board. Then to the posted schedule. Marcus's name is on Saturday.

*Record scratch.*

**EXT. WEDDING RECEPTION – SATURDAY**

*Wedding music plays inside.* Marcus, in his delivery uniform, stands at the door holding a pizza box. The BRIDE stares at him.

> **BRIDE**
> …Marcus?

> **MARCUS**
> That'll be $18.50.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** *Conflicts get lost. Weekends get ruined.*

**QUICK CUTS – THE APP**

*Upbeat music kicks in.*

Marcus taps his phone: *Add conflict → Saturday → Whole day → "Sister's wedding" → Submit.* A "Submitted" check pops up. *Ding.*

> **VOICEOVER**
> With Shiftwise, employees enter their own conflicts.

The manager, relaxed now with a slice of pizza, taps **Generate schedule**. The whole week fills in instantly.

> **VOICEOVER**
> The app builds the schedule around them, flags anything it can't fill…

A flag at the top reads "1 late conflict." The manager taps **Publish**. *Ding.*

> **VOICEOVER**
> …and you get the final say.

Behind the manager, the red-string board falls off the wall. *Thud.*

**INT. WEDDING RECEPTION – NIGHT**

Marcus, now in a suit, dances badly on the dance floor.

**CUT TO:** App logo.

> **VOICEOVER**
> Shiftwise. Hours of scheduling, done in minutes.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** *Your conflicts, on record. Your weekends, back.*

*Music ends on a clean hit.*

## Length
30 seconds.
