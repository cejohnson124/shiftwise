# Web page

## Audience
Primary: owners and managers (they decide and roll it out to staff).
Secondary: employees (who can bring Shiftwise to their manager).

## Headline
Shiftwise: Smarter shifts, fewer headaches

## Subhead
Build the week's schedule in minutes, not hours.

## The problem, in the user's words
Manager: "Making the schedule eats hours every week, and it's not just filling slots. I'm juggling who works well with who, and all the people stuff that comes with it."
Employee: "I am not always able to get the hours I need at the times I want because of conflicts and management decisions."
(Last-minute call-offs are out of scope. The app can't prevent them.)

## How it works
1. Managers set the hours each employee needs per week, plus any extra hours they want.
2. Employees enter conflicts (times they can't work), one-time or repeating, by the day before the schedule is made.
3. Shiftwise builds a draft schedule on its own around every conflict, puts everyone on at least once, fills each shift, and flags anything it can't fill.
4. The manager reviews the draft and approves it.

## How the app helps
- Cuts schedule-making time from hours to minutes.
- Frees managers to focus on running the business instead of the spreadsheet.
- Lets employees know their voice is heard: their conflicts are on record and built into the schedule.

- Colors: navy and amber.
- Style: friendly and rounded.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (main) + Caveat (handwritten accents). No Comic Sans.
- Tone: professional but warm.
- Images: none yet (to be added later), but the page shouldn't look plain or blank.
