How Shiftwise works

Four simple steps. Shiftwise does the heavy lifting, and you stay in charge.

1
Manager

Set weekly hours

Enter the hours each employee needs each week, plus any extra hours they want.

2
Employees

Enter conflicts

Employees add any day or time they can't work, one time or every week, by the day before the schedule is made.

3
Shiftwise

Build the draft

Shiftwise schedules around every conflict, puts everyone on, fills each shift, and flags anything it can't fill.

4
Manager

Review & approve

Look it over, make any tweaks, and publish. You get the final say.
